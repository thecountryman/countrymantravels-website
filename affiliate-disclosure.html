How this site is funded

Affiliate Disclosure

Effective August 2, 2026

The short version: Some links are affiliate links. If you book or buy through one, Countryman Travels may earn a commission, typically without changing the price you pay. Affiliate relationships do not purchase favorable coverage.

How affiliate links work

A booking, ticket, shopping, credit-card, or other commercial link may contain tracking information identifying Countryman Travels as the referring publisher. If you complete a qualifying action, the merchant or affiliate network may pay us. Eligibility, attribution windows, and commission amounts are controlled by the partner and can change.

How links are labeled

Pages containing affiliate recommendations display a short disclosure before or next to the relevant commercial links. Links may also use technical attributes that identify them as sponsored. A link to this full disclosure in the footer is supplemental and does not replace the nearby disclosure.

Editorial independence

Recommendations are selected for usefulness, fit, availability, and reader value. Compensation may affect which merchants can be linked, but it does not guarantee inclusion, ranking, a positive review, or an “our pick” designation. Countryman Travels may link to a non-affiliate official source when it is the clearest or safest option.

Prices, inventory, and transactions

Travel prices, ticket inventory, fees, cancellation terms, and promotions change frequently. Verify all details on the seller's website before purchase. Transactions are completed with the external merchant, not Countryman Travels, and are subject to that merchant's terms and support policies.

Guidebooks and QR links

Printed guidebooks may use permanent Countryman Travels web or QR routes that lead to current resources. The destination of those routes may be updated when offers expire, providers change, or a more useful option becomes available. The landing page will disclose any affiliate relationship before the commercial link.

Travel and financial information

Travel guidance is general information, not a guarantee of availability or suitability. Credit-card and rewards content is educational and is not individualized financial advice. Issuer terms control; review current rates, fees, benefits, eligibility rules, and required disclosures before applying.

Questions

Questions about a recommendation or commercial relationship can be directed to Countryman Travels through the current contact method listed on the About page.
