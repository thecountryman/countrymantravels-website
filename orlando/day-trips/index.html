Plan the decision

Four directions for a genuinely wanted non-Disney day

Each costs a full day plus transportation. On a short first Disney trip, rest may still be the better choice.

Space Coast

Kennedy Space Center

A substantial day trip with timed experiences, a long drive, and enough content to deserve the entire day.

Nature

Central Florida springs

Swimming, paddling, wildlife, and seasonal access. Confirm reservations, water conditions, and transportation.

Atlantic or Gulf

Beach day

Choose a coast deliberately, then account for traffic, parking, weather, equipment, and the return drive.

Orlando

Other parks and attractions

Universal receives its own Countryman Travels guide soon. Do not squeeze another major park into a rest day.
