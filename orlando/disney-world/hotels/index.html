Plan the decision

Choose the zone first

The shortest route home at night usually matters more than the prettiest lobby photograph.

Zone A

Seven Seas Lagoon & Bay Lake

Choose it when Magic Kingdom is the center of the trip or a midday return with young children must be genuinely easy.

Zone B

Crescent Lake

Choose it when walking to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios—and walking home from dinner—matters most.

Zone C

The Skyliner line

Choose it for gondola access to two parks at prices ranging from value to deluxe villas.

Zone D

Animal Kingdom & the south

Choose it when budget or Animal Kingdom matters more than avoiding buses.

Zone E

Disney Springs & Sassagoula

Choose it for quieter grounds, villa space, kitchens, and Disney Springs access.
