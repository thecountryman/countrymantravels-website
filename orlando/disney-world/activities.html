Rest days and add-ons

An add-on is a treat only when getting there is easy.

Pools, mini golf, water parks, boat rentals, campfires, spas, golf, cruises, and tours can be the best afternoon of the trip. None is compulsory.

Plan the decision

Choose by energy—not by fear of missing out

Protect the recovery block first, then add only the activity that improves it.

Easy

Resort pool and slow morning

The first-timer default. It is close, flexible, and often delivers most of what the group needs.

Short

Mini golf

Useful for a spare evening or children who still need to move. Heat and rain still matter.

Full day

Water parks

Confirm which park operates, the forecast, admission, transportation, and whether this replaces a theme-park day.

Quiet

Boat rental, spa, or golf

Best when the resort or rest day already makes access straightforward. Reserve ahead where required.

Premium

Fireworks cruise or guided tour

Worth considering when it is the trip’s chosen once-only moment—not another item squeezed into the schedule.

Included

Campfire or outdoor movie

A strong young-family evening when the hosting resort and current schedule make it easy.
