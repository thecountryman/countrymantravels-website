Packing and useful gear

Two lists. One lighter park bag.

What travels to Florida is not the same as what should be carried for fourteen hours.

In the suitcase

Recover and rotate

  • Broken-in walking shoes plus a second pair
  • Light clothes for heat and sudden rain
  • A layer for aggressive air conditioning
  • Labelled prescription medication
  • Blister care and basic health supplies
  • Swimwear only when the pool is in the plan
  • A laundry plan for longer stays

In the park bag

Carry the day

  • Phone, charging battery, and cable
  • ID and backup payment method
  • Required medication
  • Refillable bottle and sunscreen
  • Light rain protection
  • A safe snack for dietary needs
  • Stroller marker and child contact card
Do not pack from memory alone

Review current bag, cooler, stroller, and prohibited-item rules shortly before departure. If the “just in case” bag becomes painful, use a locker or return to the hotel.
