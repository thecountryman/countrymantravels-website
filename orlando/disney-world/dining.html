Dining without overplanning

Book the meals that are hard to replace.

One meaningful reservation plus flexible quick service is easier, cheaper, and kinder to a day that changes.

Living companionBook strategy from the guide. Current links and decision tools here.Last editorial review: August 2026

Plan the decision

Use each meal for one job

A reservation should solve a real need: a celebration, a character priority, a long indoor rest, or a scarce experience.

Flexible

Quick service

Usually the right lunch default. Mobile order before everyone is hungry, then stay near the area already in the plan.

Rest

Table service

Buys a seat, air conditioning, and a real pause—but also creates a fixed appointment and cancellation exposure.

Priority

Character dining

Efficient when meeting characters is central to the trip. Treat the lineup and experience as changeable.

Prepayment

Dining plans

Useful for some families, but not automatically a discount. Compare with what the group will realistically eat.

The 60-day strategy

Book the hardest-to-replace meal first. Keep reservations inside or near the park already planned for that day, review cancellation terms, and preserve at least one flexible meal most days.
