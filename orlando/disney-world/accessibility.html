Individual needs

Plan for the traveler—not an imaginary average guest.

Mobility, sensory needs, medical care, food, communication, and rest should be part of the trip design before reservations are fixed.

Living companionBook strategy from the guide. Current links and decision tools here.Last editorial review: August 2026

Plan the decision

Build one shared needs summary

Write down what every adult must know so one person is not responsible for remembering everything.

Mobility

Check transfers, boarding, room-to-lobby distance, bus stops, and whether a wheelchair or ECV extends the traveler’s enjoyable day.

Sensory planning

Identify noise, darkness, motion, crowds, scents, touch, and surprise effects. Schedule recovery before overload.

Food and medical needs

Review official information, talk with the dining team, carry necessary supplies, and keep one known-safe backup item.

Babies and children

Locate Baby Care Centers, measure height without shoes, prepare Rider Switch alternatives, and teach the lost-child plan.

Strollers

Choose bring or rent based on stamina, airport logistics, folding, transportation, and whether the stroller must return to the hotel.

Queue tools

Rider Switch, Single Rider, Queue Re-entry, and DAS solve different needs. Eligibility and process must be verified officially.
