🌆 Downtown & Fremont Street

🚌 Free Transit & Shuttles: Downtown Loop (Free shuttle connecting Fremont, Mob Museum, Arts District, and North Outlets). Running daily.
🅿️ Best Parking Garages: Garage at 500 Fremont ($15/day), Neonopolis Garage ($3/hr, $15 max), Golden Nugget (Validated with dining).
📍 Top Highlights: Fremont Street Experience Canopy, Mob Museum, Neon Museum Boneyard, Circa Stadium Swim.

🎨 Las Vegas Arts District (18b)

🚌 Transit & Rideshare: RTC Deuce Bus (Arts District Stop on Charleston & Main). Rideshare dropoff at Main St & Coolidge.
🅿️ Parking: Free & metered street parking along Main St & Commerce St ($2/hr). Metered lot at Art Square.
📍 Top Highlights: Hop Highway Microbreweries (Able Baker, HUDL), Velveteen Rabbit, Antique Alley Mall, First Friday Festival.

🍜 Chinatown & Spring Mountain Road

🚌 Getting There: 5-minute rideshare from Center Strip ($10-$14). RTC Bus Route 203 along Spring Mountain Rd.
🅿️ Parking: Free surface lot parking in Chinatown Plaza, Shanghai Plaza, and Spring Mountain Center.
📍 Top Culinary Hotspots: Shanghai Plaza, Golden Tiki Speakeasy, Raku Robata, Lotus of Siam (Flamingo branch), Sweets Raku.
