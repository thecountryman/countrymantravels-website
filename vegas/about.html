About This Guide

A guidebook that gets rewritten, not just reprinted.

Las Vegas changes faster than a printed guide can keep up with. Countryman's Vegas Right Now separates durable planning advice from volatile prices, offers, hours, and lineups — every time-sensitive item on this site is dated, and every listing points back to the official source before you pay or travel.

No business paid for placement in this guide. A listing is not a guarantee of quality, availability, safety, or accessibility. Prices exclude tax, tips, service charges, and add-ons unless stated otherwise.

Editorial independence
Every fact on this site is verified against an official source and dated. If it's changed since, confirm directly with the venue before paying or traveling.
