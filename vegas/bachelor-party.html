GROUP TRIPS · BACHELOR & BOYS' TRIP HQ

The Las Vegas Bachelor Party, Planned Like an Adult

A Vegas group trip fails in predictable ways: no anchor reservations, a hotel that fights the plan, and a budget nobody agreed on. This page is the fix — where to stay for groups, what to lock in before you land, and the experiences that are actually worth group money. No hype, real costs.

STEP 1 · BASE CAMP

🏨 Best all-around group bases

Palms (big suites, off-Strip pricing), ARIA (center-Strip, connected suites), and The LINQ (cheap, dead-center, nobody cares about noise). Check each field-guide page for resort fees and parking before you commit.

💵 Budget move

Downtown: Circa is adults-only with a sportsbook pool and rates that leave money for the nights. Compare true costs on the resort-fee table.

🎯 The rule

Pick ONE base. Split groups across two hotels lose 45 minutes to every regroup. Book rooms on the same floor if you can, and confirm the hotel's party/guest policy before paying.

STEP 2 · BOOK THE ANCHORS

Lock one anchor per day before the trip — one night thing, one day thing. Everything else stays loose. These are the group experiences that consistently deliver for stag groups:

🚌 VIP Club Crawl + Party Bus

Express entry at multiple clubs with a host, drinks, and transport handled — the easiest way to run a big night for a group without cover-charge chaos.

STEP 3 · NIGHTS

🎤 A show that works for groups

Absinthe at Caesars is the default bachelor-group pick — crude, close-up, and nobody checks their phone. Read the planning notes or browse the full shows directory.

🍸 Nightlife strategy

Club tables get expensive fast ($1,000+ minimums on weekends). For most groups the crawl-with-host format beats one table — see the club crawl above, and the nightlife guide for the club-by-club rundown.

🃏 The cheap great night

A $200-per-head casino night at a downtown card room out-delivers a $200 cover at a megaclub for a lot of groups. Casino basics here — set loss limits before drinking, not after.

STEP 4 · MONEY & LOGISTICS

💰 Agree the number first

Set a per-head budget covering room share, two anchors, and a nightly pool — before anyone books flights. Our costs & tipping guide has the real per-day numbers.

🚕 Getting around

Rideshare from the airport, walk or monorail on the Strip, and one designated booker for every reservation. Details in getting here & around.

🗓️ When to go

Shoulder months (late Apr–May, Sep–Oct) get pool weather without July pricing or heat. Check the month-by-month planner.
