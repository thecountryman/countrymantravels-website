FIELD NOTES

🍽️ On-Property Dining

The Steak House (Award-winning prime dry-aged beef), Pizzeria, Cravings Food Court, Vince Neil's Tatuado Eat-Drink-Party.

🎭 Shows & Nightlife

Free World-Class Circus Acts (Daily starting 11:30 AM), Midway Carnival Games.

🏊 Pool Deck

Splash Zone pool complex with 50-foot water slide tower and splash pad.

📍 Transit & Access

RTC Deuce Bus Stop directly outside main entrance; 10-min walk to Convention Center.

📍 Nearby (Walking Distance)

Fontainebleau, Resorts World, Sahara, STRAT.
