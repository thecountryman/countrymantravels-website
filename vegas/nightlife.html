Chapter 13

Nightclubs and dayclubs

The best night begins with a budget, transport plan, and clear consent — not a guest-list promise.

🎉 Planning a bachelor or group trip? Start with the bachelor party playbook.

Before you go

  • Confirm dress code, cover, entry cutoff, ID rules, bag policy, and whether guest-list entry is guaranteed.
  • For tables or cabanas, request the complete minimum-spend calculation, service charge, tax, and gratuity policy in writing.
  • Never leave a drink unattended; stay with your group and use official pickup zones.
  • Hydrate before and during pool events. Desert sun and alcohol compound risk.
  • Decide the return ride before entering; event egress can produce long waits and surge pricing.
