Chapter 19

Final checklists

The small checks prevent the expensive mistakes.

72 hours before departure

  • Reconfirm flights, room, airport transfer, shows, restaurants, and accessibility arrangements.
  • Check weather, road alerts, venue bag rules, and outdoor closures.
  • Download confirmations, maps, and transit tickets for offline access.
  • Review cancellation deadlines and identify flexible items.
  • Notify banks if needed; set gaming and discretionary spending limits.

At hotel check-in

  • Confirm room type, nightly rate, mandatory fees, deposit, parking, and checkout time.
  • Ask where rideshare, taxis, transit, and accessible entrances are located.
  • Photograph existing room damage and report issues promptly.
  • Store valuables appropriately and keep identification secure.

Before checkout

  • Review the folio line by line.
  • Resolve duplicate, minibar, parking, or credit errors before departure.
  • Allow airport time for traffic, vehicle pickup, terminal transfer, and security.
  • Keep receipts and confirmations until all card charges settle. Leave room for sleep, water, changing your mind, and one unexpected discovery.
