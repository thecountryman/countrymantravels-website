💵 Las Vegas Tipping Quick Reference

🧹 Housekeeping: $3 to $5 per night, left daily on nightstand.
🚗 Valet Attendants: $3 to $5 upon vehicle pickup.
🍸 Bartenders & Cocktail Servers: $1 to $2 per drink (especially for complimentary casino floor drinks!).
🧳 Bellhop / Luggage: $2 to $3 per bag delivered to room.
🚖 Rideshare & Taxi: 15% to 20% of total fare.
