✈️ Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) Pickup Zones

  • Terminal 1 Rideshare (Uber/Lyft): Level 2 of Parking Garage. Follow signs up escalator across bridge.
  • Terminal 3 Rideshare: Valet Level (Level V) of Parking Garage.
  • Airport Taxis: Flat rate zones ($22-$32 to Strip zones + $3 airport surcharge).

🚆 Strip Monorail & Bus Transit

  • Las Vegas Monorail: $6 single ride / $13 pass. Stations at MGM Grand, Horseshoe/Paris, Flamingo, LINQ, Harrah's, Westgate, SAHARA.
  • RTC Deuce Bus: $6 2-hr pass / $8 24-hr pass. Runs 24/7 along Las Vegas Blvd from South Strip to Downtown Fremont.
