## Chapter 1: The 5 Core Trip Decisions

To build a flawless Las Vegas vacation, you must answer five key questions before checking out on any booking platform:

1. **Where will your daily anchor be?** (South Strip, Mid-Strip, North Strip, Fremont/Downtown, or Off-Strip)
2. **What is your true daily budget?** (Base room rate + resort fee + food/beverage + transit + entertainment)

5. **What is your trip pacing?** (High-energy nightlife vs. relaxed pool-and-spa retreat)

## Chapter 2: Las Vegas Neighborhoods & Hotel Zones

Understanding the geography of Las Vegas is the single most effective way to protect your trip budget and energy.

```
       [ NORTH STRIP ]        -> STRAT, Sahara, Fontainebleau, Resorts World, Circus Circus
             |
       [ MID-STRIP ]          -> Caesars Palace, Bellagio, Venetian/Palazzo, Mirage/Hard Rock, Cosmopolitan
             |
       [ SOUTH STRIP ]        -> MGM Grand, NY-NY, Park MGM, Luxor, Mandalay Bay, Excalibur
             |
     [ DOWNTOWN / FREMONT ]   -> Circa, Golden Nugget, Plaza, El Cortez, D Las Vegas
```

### 1. Mid-Strip (The Heart of Action)
* **Best for:** First-timers, walking convenience, access to iconic fountains and central show venues.
* **Key Properties:** Bellagio, Caesars Palace, The Venetian & Palazzo, The Cosmopolitan, Paris Las Vegas.
* **Pros:** Unmatched walkability to world-class dining, central monorail station, central location.
* **Cons:** Highest average room rates and heaviest pedestrian traffic.

### 2. South Strip (Resort Hubs & Sports Access)
* **Best for:** Families, budget-conscious travelers, sports fans (Allegiant Stadium / T-Mobile Arena).
* **Key Properties:** MGM Grand, New York-New York, Park MGM (smoke-free), Luxor, Mandalay Bay, Excalibur.
* **Pros:** Excellent pool complexes (Mandalay Beach, MGM Grand Pool District), smoke-free options (Park MGM), direct stadium proximity.
* **Cons:** Longer walks to Mid-Strip destinations; heavy congestion on event nights.

### 3. North Strip (Modern Luxury & Event Venues)
* **Best for:** Sphere event attendees, convention goers, modern luxury lovers.
* **Key Properties:** Fontainebleau Las Vegas, Resorts World, Wynn & Encore, SAHARA, The STRAT.
* **Pros:** Brand-new luxury, quieter casino floors, state-of-the-art dining, proximity to Sphere.
* **Cons:** Spaced-out properties requiring transit between resorts.

### 4. Downtown Las Vegas / Fremont Street
* **Best for:** Value seekers, vintage Vegas vibes, lower table minimums, walkable nightlife.
* **Key Properties:** Circa Resort (21+ only), Golden Nugget, Plaza, Downtown Grand, El Cortez.
* **Pros:** Lower minimum bets ($5-$10 blackjack), vibrant street energy under the Viva Vision canopy, compact footprint.
* **Cons:** High noise levels until early morning hours; 15-minute cab ride from the main Strip.

## Chapter 4: The Real Cost of Vegas & Resort Fee Cheat Sheet

### What is a Mandatory Resort Fee?
A resort fee is a daily charge added by the property upon check-in, separate from room rate and taxes. It typically covers:
- In-room Wi-Fi
- Fitness center access
- Local phone calls
- Select pool amenity access

> **WARNING:** Resort fees are subject to 13.38% Clark County lodging tax! An advertised $49 room rate with a $45 resort fee actually costs **~$106 per night** before parking or incidental deposits.

### Transportation Cost Comparison
- **Walking:** Free! Expect 10,000 to 20,000 steps per day.
- **Las Vegas Monorail:** $5.50 single ride; $13.45 1-day pass; $29.75 3-day pass. Connects MGM Grand to SAHARA on the east side of the Strip.
- **Deuce Bus:** $6 for 2-hour pass; $8 for 24-hour pass. Runs 24/7 along Las Vegas Boulevard from South Strip to Downtown.
- **Rideshare (Uber/Lyft):** $15–$35 for typical Strip trips; $40–$75 during high-surge event hours (Sphere/Concerts).
- **Taxi:** Standard flat rates from Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) to Strip zones ($21–$30 flat rate plus credit card fee & tip).

Las Vegas is the entertainment capital of the world. Booking your tickets in advance guarantees seats for high-demand shows.

### 1. Cirque du Soleil Productions
- **"O" at Bellagio:** Aquatic masterpiece built inside a 1.5-million-gallon pool. Ideal for couples and theater enthusiasts.
- **Michael Jackson ONE at Mandalay Bay:** High-energy acrobatics fused with MJ's legendary choreography and state-of-the-art surround sound.
- **KÀ at MGM Grand:** Epic martial-arts battle featuring a rotating 360-degree vertical stage platform.
- **Mad Apple at New York-New York:** A comedy, music, and acrobatics fusion with NYC bar energy.

### 2. Sphere & Immersive Media
- **The Sphere:** The world's largest spherical structure featuring a 16K wrap-around LED screen and haptic seats.
- **Featured Shows:** The Wizard of Oz immersive experience, Postcard from Earth, and major rock residencies (e.g., U2, Eagles, Phish, Dead & Company).

### 3. Comedy & Magic
- **Shin Lim:** Mind-bending sleight of hand card magic at The Venetian.
- **Piff the Magic Dragon:** Hilarious comedy-magic at Flamingo.
- **Brad Garrett's Comedy Club:** Classic stand-up in the basement lounge at MGM Grand.
- **Absinthe at Caesars Palace:** Rated #1 adult comedy and cabaret circus show in Las Vegas (21+ only).

## Chapter 6: Attractions, Museums & Iconic Experiences

### Must-Visit Vegas Experiences
1. **High Roller Observation Wheel (The LINQ Promenade):** 550-foot wheel offering 360-degree views of the Strip. (Pro tip: Happy Half Hour cabin includes an open bar).
2. **The Mob Museum (Downtown):** National Museum of Organized Crime and Law Enforcement, complete with an underground speakeasy distillery.
3. **The Neon Museum:** The "Neon Boneyard" housing over 200 historic vintage neon signs from classic Vegas casinos.
4. **AREA15 & Meow Wolf's Omega Mart:** Surreal, interactive art installation disguised as an otherworldly supermarket.
5. **Fremont Street Experience:** Free light-and-sound shows on the world's largest video screen suspended over pedestrian street.

Take a break from the casino floor and explore Nevada's natural wonderlands:

```
[ LAS VEGAS STRIP ]
   |-- 25 mins West  --> Red Rock Canyon (Scenic Loop, Rock Climbing, Hiking)
   |-- 45 mins South --> Hoover Dam & Lake Mead (Powerplant Tour, Kayaking)
   |-- 45 mins South --> Seven Magic Mountains (Art Installation in Desert)
```

- **Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area:** A 13-mile scenic drive with dramatic red sandstone formations and miles of hiking trails.
- **Hoover Dam & Emerald Cave:** Tour the massive engineering marvel or take a guided kayak tour through the crystal-clear waters of Black Canyon.
- **Valley of Fire State Park:** Aztec sandstone rock formations dating back 150 million years, located 1 hour northeast of the Strip.

## Chapter 9: Nevada Cannabis Laws & Visitor Protocol

> **CAUTION:** While recreational cannabis is legal in Nevada for adults 21+, strict consumption laws apply to tourists:

1. **Where to Buy:** Purchase only from licensed dispensaries (e.g., Planet 13, Reef, Cultivate). Keep state ID or passport handy.
2. **Where Public Consumption is Forbidden:** It is **illegal** to smoke, vape, or consume edibles in hotel rooms, casino floors, streets, sidewalks, parking garages, or cars.
3. **Where to Consume:** Legal consumption is limited to private residences or state-licensed **Cannabis Consumption Lounges** (such as Dazed Lounge at Planet 13).
4. **Federal & Airport Warning:** Federal law prohibits cannabis on airport grounds or across state lines. Dispose of product before arriving at LAS airport.

## Chapter 10: Tipping Protocol & Casino Etiquette

### Tipping Standards
- **Casino Dealers:** $5-$10 per session, or place a $1-$5 bet for the dealer alongside your hand.
- **Cocktail Servers:** $2 per drink on casino floors (drinks are complimentary while playing, but tipping keeps service fast!).
- **Valet Parking:** $5-$10 upon retrieving vehicle.
- **Housekeeping:** $3-$5 per day left on desk/nightstand.
- **Luggage Bellhop:** $2-$3 per bag.

### Casino Etiquette Fundamentals
- **Tipping for drinks:** Always tip cash when beverage servers bring your free drink order while gambling.
- **Hand Signals:** In Blackjack, use tap-for-hit and wave-for-stand. Dealers cannot accept verbal calls.
- **Cash Transfers:** Place cash flat on the felt table; dealers cannot take money directly from your hand.

## Chapter 12: Vegas Weddings & Vow Renewals

Las Vegas performs over 70,000 weddings annually!

- **Marriage License:** Obtain in-person at the Clark County Marriage License Bureau (Downtown Vegas). No waiting period or blood tests required!
- **Chapel Options:**
