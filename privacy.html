Legal and transparency

Privacy Policy

Effective August 2, 2026

In plain English: Countryman Travels uses ordinary hosting and analytics information to operate and improve this website. We do not sell personal information. When you follow an external booking or shopping link, the destination company's privacy and cookie practices apply.

Information collected

Our hosting, security, and analytics providers may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, approximate location, referring page, pages viewed, link clicks, and timestamps. If you voluntarily contact us or join a future mailing list, we may process the information you choose to provide, such as your name and email address.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate and secure the site, understand which guides and tools are useful, diagnose errors, measure traffic and outbound-link performance, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations. We do not use submitted contact information to send marketing unless you expressly request or consent to it.

Cookies and analytics

The site may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, security, preferences, and affiliate attribution. You can limit cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may prevent an affiliate partner from crediting Countryman Travels for a qualifying transaction.

Affiliate and external websites

Some links lead to independent companies that provide hotels, tickets, tours, merchandise, insurance, or other travel services. Those companies may set cookies or collect information after you visit their websites. Countryman Travels does not control their checkout systems, privacy practices, prices, inventory, or transaction records. Review the destination site's policies before providing personal or payment information.

Service providers and disclosure of information

We may use service providers for hosting, security, analytics, email, and affiliate measurement. They may process information only as needed to provide those services or as required by law. Information may also be disclosed to protect users, investigate fraud or security incidents, enforce applicable terms, or comply with a valid legal request.

Retention and choices

Technical and analytics information is retained only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, subject to provider settings and legal requirements. You may use browser privacy controls, opt-out tools supplied by analytics or advertising providers, and the privacy controls offered by any external partner you visit.

Children

This site is intended for general travel planning and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the site, service providers, and legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be directed to Countryman Travels through the current contact method listed on the About page. We may need enough information to understand and respond to a request.
